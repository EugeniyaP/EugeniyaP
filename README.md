### Dr. Eugenia J. Perekhodko
**Computational Social Scientist & Algorithmic Graph Theorist**  
Ph.D. in Engineering & Computer Science (*University of Rome "Tor Vergata"*) | NetSI Alumna

I bridge algorithmic graph theory and structural sociology to model relation-centric network resilience, elite closure, and longitudinal core dynamics in asymmetric social and cyber ecosystems.

- **Research Focus:** Directed Structural Adhesion ($\lambda$-SA), Dynamic Network Analysis (DNA), Massive Compressed Graphs, Social Cybersecurity.
- **Tech Stack:** Java (`WebGraph`), C++, Python (`NetworkX`, `SciPy`, `Statsmodels`), R.
- **Selected Publications:**
  - *Local 2-connected bow-tie structure of the Web and of social networks* — **Applied Network Science** 9(1), 34 (2024) [[DOI]](https://doi.org)
  - *The Dynamics of Asymmetry: Temporal Evolution of 2-Edge-Strongly-Connected Cores in Knowledge Exchange Networks* (2026)
  - *The Architecture of Asymmetry: Directed Structural Adhesion and Elite Closure in Interaction Networks* — **SocArXiv** [[Preprint]](https://osf.io)
  - *What's inside a bow-tie: analyzing the core of the web and of social networks* — **ICISDM '17** (with G. F. Italiano & N. Parotsidis)
